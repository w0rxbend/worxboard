# JLCCNC order: the machined case parts

This folder is the CNC order for one keyboard: 4 case parts + 6 legs, aluminium. Every file except
this page is generated by `just model order production` from `case/worxboard_case.py` and
`case/drawings.py` (case revision **A5**). CI fails if they drift from the model
(`just production-check`). The printed parts are in [`../jlc3dp/`](../jlc3dp/ORDER_NOTES.md), the
screws, feet and leg hardware in [`../hardware.csv`](../hardware.csv). To order the same files at
PCBWay instead, see [`../pcbway/`](../pcbway/ORDER_NOTES.md).

## Files: one upload per part

JLCCNC wants one part per STEP file and a 2D drawing **with the same file name** next to it. The
STEP governs the geometry, the PDF governs threads, tolerances and finish.

| Upload | STEP + PDF | Qty | Size (mm) | Threads |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Part 1 | `worxboard_right_top` (drawing WXB-TOP-R, 2 sheets) | 1 | 192.4 × 165.6 × 12.6 | 8 × M2.5 + 3 × M3 |
| Part 2 | `worxboard_left_top` (WXB-TOP-L, 2 sheets) | 1 | 192.4 × 165.6 × 12.6 | 8 × M2.5 + 3 × M3 |
| Part 3 | `worxboard_right_bottom` (WXB-BOTTOM-R) | 1 | 189.7 × 165.6 × 7.0 | none |
| Part 4 | `worxboard_left_bottom` (WXB-BOTTOM-L) | 1 | 189.7 × 165.6 × 7.0 | none |
| Part 5 | `worxboard_leg` (WXB-LEG) | **6** | 10.4 × 48.7 × 8.0 | none |

`hole_table.csv` lists every hole, thread and feature in STEP coordinates (keep it for questions).
Do not upload the `assembly_with_legs` STEP from `case/exports`: JLCCNC cannot quote assemblies.
The left parts are mirror images of the right ones; mirror the geometry only, **never the threads**.

## Form settings

| Option | Tops (parts 1, 2) | Bottoms (parts 3, 4) | Leg (part 5) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Process | CNC milling | CNC milling | CNC milling |
| Material | Aluminium 6061-T6 (7075-T6 gives a stiffer plate) | Aluminium 6061-T6 | Aluminium 6061-T6 |
| Surface finish | the anodizing option **with** bead blasting (plain anodizing has no blasting) | same as the tops | same as the tops |
| Appearance standard | **Strict** (or Premium), if the form offers it | same | same |
| Threads | **Yes: 11 per part, 8 × M2.5 + 3 × M3, all right-hand; T1 and T5 through, the other 9 blind** | No | No |
| Tolerance | ISO 2768-m (default); tighter values only where the drawing gives them: hole patterns ±0.1, cavity wall +0.2/0, boss heights, slot widths (general notes, "FIT-CRITICAL") | same: hole pattern ±0.1, post tops | same |
| Quantity | 1 each | 1 each | 6 |

Add a remark that the parts must fit together (JLCCNC then checks the fits), and one about a
uniform anodize colour across all 10 parts. A remark does not change the appearance tier, though:
JLCCNC's [Appearance Standard Guidelines](https://jlccnc.com/help/article/appearance-standard-guidelines)
allow "slight anodizing color variation among some products" in the Standard tier and "no visible
color variation" only in Strict and Premium. Pick **Strict** where the form offers the tier, so the
two halves and the six legs match (deviation ORDER-9).

## What changed for this order

Case revision **A5** (pre-order mechanical review, deviation CASE-10):

- **The USB-C and TRRS openings are U-slots, open to the parting face.** J1 is a 13.0 mm wide slot
  through the whole back wall, Z 6.2–14.2 (its lowest 0.8 mm is a notch through the tray wall); it
  replaces the 9.4 × 3.6 notch behind a 1.8 mm deep recess, which stopped a plug's overmould 1.7 mm
  before the receptacle. J2 is an 8.2 mm wide slot through the inner bump wall, Z 7.0–15.2; it replaces
  the Ø6.5 hole. Both are cut from the underside: **no side operation is left except the three leg
  bosses.** The J2 slot runs 1.5 mm past the wall as a 0.6 mm step in the pocket ceiling, with R1.5
  end corners.
- **T1 and T5 are tapped through.** The two bosses under the key well were blind, 3.6 deep, and held
  1.4 mm of an M2.5 × 10. They are now drilled and tapped through the boss and the plate (5.0 mm) and
  exit in the key-well floor between two keycaps, with a Ø2.9 × 90° chamfer. All eight holes take an
  M2.5 × 12, which engages 3.4 mm.
- **The key well keeps 1.75 mm of wall** (was 2.0) at the two thumb-cluster corners where it is
  clipped, so the T0 and T3 keycaps clear the well wall by 0.77 mm (was 0.52).
- **Tolerances on the fit-critical dimensions** in the drawings' general notes: hole patterns ±0.1,
  cavity wall +0.2/0, boss face to plate top 5.00 ±0.10, parting face to boss face 1.60 +0.10/0, slot
  widths +0.2/0, plate flatness in the restrained state, dimensions after finishing, overall sizes.
- The bottoms change only at the USB slot; the leg is as in A4 (new revision letter and the
  after-finishing note only).

Case revision **A4** (worxboard rev B, deviation REVB-2):

- **The display moves 1.0 mm back.** The pocket's back end, the raised bezel's back and the 12 × 26 mm
  viewing window move 1.0 mm towards the back edge with the nice!view and its J3 socket, so the module
  no longer reaches the C6R1 key well. The pocket's far end, in the key well, is where it was. Only the
  two top parts change; the bottoms and the legs are as in A3 (their drawings carry the new revision
  letter only).

Case revision **A3** (TRRS jack clearance):

- **The TRRS jack's relief round J2 is 0.25 mm wider on every side.** It runs through the plate
  between the C6R1 and C6R2 switch cut-outs, and in A2 its R1.5 corners came within 0.02 mm of the
  jack body's far corners, so the plate edge could bear on the jack once the case is screwed shut.
  The jack body now keeps 0.75 mm on its sides and at least 0.38 mm at its corners; the plate keeps
  4.4 mm to the switch cut-outs. Only the two top parts change; the bottoms and the leg are as in A2
  (their drawings carry the new revision letter only).

Case revision **A2** (corners):

- **Every internal corner at JLCCNC's minimum radius for its depth** (your CNC Machining Design
  Guideline: R = H/10 + 0.5 with a Ø H/5 tool; past 10 mm the Min R of the tool in your table that
  reaches). The display pocket D1 is 11.6 mm deep from the parting face, past the Ø2 tool's 10 mm, so
  its two back corners now have **R2 dog-bones** for the Ø3 tool (were R1.5). The pad and web fillets
  and the relief pockets above the plate are R1.5 (were R1.0 or sharp), the bottom web fillets R1.0
  (were R0.5). A boss that stood 1.0 mm into the key well beside T2 is cut back flush with the well.
  `just corners` now checks every internal corner of the STEPs against this rule.

Case revision **A1**:

- **Display bezel B1.** The display socket J3 is now the 5.0 mm socket JLCPCB fits, and a stock
  nice!view plugs into it on its own header. Its glass then sits 8.9 mm above the PCB, 9.75 mm at
  full tolerance: above the old top face. The display pocket D1 now rises to Z 18.6, under a
  1.0 mm skin in a bezel that stands **2.0 mm above the top face** (Z 19.6). The top part is
  therefore 12.6 mm tall instead of 10.6. Detail C on sheet 2 is a real section through it.
- **Threads per the JLCCNC guideline.** Every tapped hole is blind and keeps at least half its
  diameter unthreaded at the bottom: the drawing gives the drill depth (full diameter, the 118°
  point is extra and modelled) and the minimum full thread separately, plus the screw each hole
  takes. The screws are shorter so that they seat before reaching the end of the thread:
  M2.5 × 12 (was × 14) in T2–T4 and T6–T8, M2.5 × 10 (was × 12) in T1 and T5.

## Remarks (paste for the TOP parts)

```
Threads per the 2D drawing (sheet 1 table), ALL RIGHT-HAND:
8x M2.5x0.45-6H (T1-T8) + 3x M3x0.5-6H (L1-L3). The left part is a mirror image; its threads stay right-hand.
STEP models the holes at tap-drill size with the drill point. Drill depth = full-diameter depth.
Blind holes: per your threaded-hole guideline at least D/2 stays unthreaded at the bottom; min full thread per table:
T2-T4, T6-T8: drill 6.0, full thread 4.5. L1, L3: drill 5.5, full thread 4.0. L2: drill 7.5, full thread 6.0.
T1 and T5 are THROUGH holes (5.0 mm) that exit in the cosmetic key-well floor: tap through, chamfer the exit
dia. 2.9 x 90 deg as modelled, no burr.
Fit-critical tolerances are in the general notes: T1-T8 positions +/-0.1 hole to hole, cavity wall +0.2/0,
boss faces to plate top 5.00 +/-0.10, parting face to boss faces 1.60 +0.10/0. Dimensions apply after finishing.
Display: raised bezel B1 (2.0 above the top face), pocket D1 machined from the parting side up to 1.0 under the
bezel top (11.6 deep: R2 dog-bones at its 2 back corners for a dia. 3 tool, keep them), window W1 through
that 1.0 skin, see detail C on sheet 2.
Internal corners are modelled at your minimum radius for their depth (R = H/10 + 0.5, dia. 3 tool past 10 mm);
machine them as modelled.
Support the skin while machining the window.
USB-C slot J1 (back wall, 13.0 wide) and TRRS slot J2 (inner wall, 8.2 wide) are open to the parting face and
cut from that side through the whole wall; no side operation. Pin-holes P1-P2 dia. 3 thru.
L1-L3: plain dia. 10 bosses on the outer walls, 2.7 high, FLAT face, M3 thread in each, see sheet 2.
Switch cutouts 14.00 +0.05/-0. Stabiliser cutouts have 1.0 mm wide side notches: use a 1 mm end mill.
Cosmetic: top face, display bezel, key well, outer walls. Mask threads during anodizing, or re-tap afterwards.
```

## Prepared answers to engineering questions

| Likely question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Thread direction? | All right-hand, on the right and the left parts. Mirroring applies to the geometry only. |
| Thread depth? | Sheet 1 table and `hole_table.csv`: drill depth (full diameter) and minimum full thread per hole. Each blind hole keeps ≥ D/2 unthreaded at the bottom, as your guideline asks; a standard plug or bottoming tap reaches the full-thread depth. T1 and T5 are through holes. |
| Can the threads be shallower? | No. The screw tips reach 3.6 mm past the boss face in every T hole, 0.9 mm short of the minimum full thread of the blind ones. |
| T1 and T5 break through the key-well floor. | Intended (since A5): a blind hole there left 1.4 mm of thread engagement. The exit lies between two keycaps. Chamfer it Ø2.9 × 90° as modelled and leave no burr; the anodize covers it. |
| Side operations? | Only the three leg bosses L1–L3 (three wall directions, sheet 2). The USB-C and TRRS slots are cut from the parting face. |
| The skin over the display pocket is thin (1.0 mm). | Intended: the display glass sits 0.25–1.1 mm under it. Machine the pocket from the parting side and support the skin for the window. |
| The bezel wall on the key-well side is 1.4 mm thick. | Intended: the C5 switch column leaves no more room. It stands 6.0 mm above the key-well floor (Z 13.6–19.6) and joins the plate. The D1 dog-bone at its back end thins the bezel wall to 0.84 mm over 1 mm of height (Z 17.6–18.6, under the skin), above your 0.8 mm minimum for metal. |
| Sharp internal corners? | None outside the exceptions below. Every internal corner is modelled at or above your minimum radius for its depth, R = H/10 + 0.5 (tool Ø H/5), rounded up to the tool; past 10 mm the Min R of the tool in your table that reaches the depth. Key well R1.0 (4.0 deep). Relief pockets above the plate, and the fillets where pads and webs meet the walls, R1.5 (to 7.6 deep from the parting face). Bottom web fillets R1.0 (4.0 deep). Display pocket D1 is 11.6 deep from the parting face, past the Ø2 tool's 10 mm: its two back corners have R2 dog-bones for the Ø3 tool (Min R 2), because the display module and its socket stand 0.5 mm from both walls there, where a fillet would hit them; its front corners lie in the key well and are R2 in the plate. Exceptions: the switch and stabiliser cutouts are R0.5 (Ø1 tool: the corner is the 1.5 mm plate at the bottom of the open 4 mm key well; the stabiliser side notches are 1.0 mm wide) and the engraved side mark M1. The TRRS slot's step in the pocket ceiling has R1.5 end corners (8.2 deep from the parting face). |
| The bar between the display window and the C6R1 key well? | 5.2 mm of 1.0 mm bezel skin, open towards the key well at its front edge (the pocket's front strip lies in the well). |
| Gaps between bosses and walls? | Every boss near a wall is joined to it by a solid web. |
| Tolerances not given? | ISO 2768-m. The STEP model is the reference geometry. The fit-critical ones are in the general notes of each drawing and apply after finishing. |
| Which faces may carry anodizing rack marks? | Inside (cavity) faces only. |
| Plate flatness 0.2 on a 1.5 mm plate? | Measured in the restrained state, screwed to the bottom part. Use low blasting pressure on the plate area. |
| Text or engraving? | Only the side mark M1 on each bottom part. |
