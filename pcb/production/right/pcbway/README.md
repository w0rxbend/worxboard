## Coordinates and rotations

Both centroid files are KiCad's own position file (`kicad-cli pcb export pos --use-drill-file-origin`, millimetres) of the panel and of the board, unchanged but for the column names and the rotation written as 0-360°:

- **Origin:** the drill/place origin, which is the origin of the matching Gerbers: the panel's top-left corner for `centroid-panel.csv`, the board's coordinate origin for `centroid-bare-board.csv`. Y points up, as in the Gerbers, so the Y values are negative.
- **Panel offset:** each panel centroid is the bare-board one moved by (-15.0810, +22.6000) mm, the KiKit panel's offset, read from the panel this stage builds.
- **Mid X / Mid Y** is each footprint's KiCad origin, with no correction. For most parts it is the body centre. Not for these, whose pads are centred this far from the origin: J1 (1.5 mm), J2 (0.9 mm), J3 (5.1 mm), SK101-SK137 (3.9 mm). Place them by their pads.
- **Rotation** is KiCad's footprint angle, counter-clockwise, with KiCad's library footprint as 0°. That is not always the zero orientation of IPC-7351 or of the part on its tape (for J2, J3, SK101-SK137, U2-U7 it is not: JLC's CPL turns them by 90°, 180° or 270°), so align each part with its pads and polarity marks.
- **Bottom side** (Layer `Bottom`): X and Y are seen from the top, through the board (X not negated), in the same frame as the Gerbers. The rotation is KiCad's: seen from the top, the part is its library footprint mirrored top to bottom, then turned counter-clockwise by the Rotation value. Seen from the bottom (the board turned over about its vertical axis), it is the footprint turned counter-clockwise by **180° minus the Rotation value**, the number bottom-view tools such as JLC's use.

## Placement confirmation: what to check

- **Bottom** (C101-C137, D101-D137, LED101-LED137, SK101-SK137): each hot-swap socket's curved notch wraps the round switch-centre hole and its two terminals sit on the two pads; each diode's cathode band is at pad 1 (the ROW side).
- **U1-U7, Y1**: the pin-1 mark (dot, bevel or corner) at pad 1 and the pin-1 mark of the silkscreen; **D1**: the cathode band at pad 1.
- **J1** (USB-C) and **J2** (TRRS): the pegs in their holes, the mouths over the board edge.
- **J3**: on its five holes, on the top side.

## Checks run on these files

- Designators: the 201 parts of the JLC BOM, which are exactly the parts `design.py` fits, each with its LCSC number; every LCSC number in `design.py` has a manufacturer part number in `pcb/scripts/mpn.json` (`pcb/scripts/mpn.py check`).
- Every centroid lies inside the Edge.Cuts of its Gerber set; the panel centroids are the bare-board ones moved by (-15.0810, +22.6000) mm.
- Against the JLC CPL: the 154 parts whose footprint has no row in `pcb/scripts/jlc_rotations.csv` sit where the JLC CPL puts them (within 0.01 mm, worst 0.0066 mm, panel and bare board), and re-applying the row to the other 47 gives the JLC CPL too: the files differ only by JLC's documented corrections and bottom-side rotation.
